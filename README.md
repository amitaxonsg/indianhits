# IndianHits — AI India Song Album

A lyric-first Indian AI concept album project.

## Scope

- **10 original songs**
- **5 Tamil + 5 Hindi**
- Songs only: **lyrics and song concepts**
- **No music-video planning**
- Indian musical identity with a modern AI/technology theme
- Each track should feel different in rhythm, mood, and lyrical approach
- Positive, fun, clever, and respectful
- Celebrate Indian talent, technology, culture, ambition, and achievements
- Question **AI/marketing hype** with light humour without attacking individuals or companies
- Avoid defamatory claims, personal insults, impersonation, or implying endorsement by public figures
- References to public figures must remain factual and respectful
- Political figures, including CM Vijay if referenced, must be handled neutrally and non-partisanly

## Album Direction

Working album title:

**AI INDIA — Beats of Tomorrow**

Core idea:

> India + AI + ambition + culture + humour — celebrating real achievement while gently questioning hype.

The album should feel **Indian first**. AI is the subject and creative theme, not a replacement for Indian musical character.

## Lyric Rules

For every song:

- Lyrics must be original
- Natural Tamil or Hindi rather than awkward direct translation
- English tech words can be mixed in where they sound natural
- Strong, memorable chorus/hook
- Keep humour friendly
- No attack lyrics
- No unsupported accusations
- No fake quotes from real people
- Do not imply that a celebrity, CEO, politician, company, or artist endorses the album
- Do not imitate a living singer's exact voice or writing style
- References to real people should celebrate documented achievements or provide harmless cultural context
- Keep political references neutral and factual
- Aim for lyrics that can work independently as songs without requiring a video
